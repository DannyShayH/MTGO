This section will show the team's flow metrics, calculated automatically from the [board](https://github.com/users/DannyShayH/projects/2) and the issue timelines, with every number traceable to concrete issues.

There are no numbers yet, because they would be invented. They come with these issues:

- WIP, the ready queue and throughput: [issue #14](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/14).
- Cycle time, work item age, old and blocked work items, and data problems: [issue #16](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/16).
- Real data from the board instead of sample data: [issue #17](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/17).

The definitions of every metric are in [`GLOSSARY.md`](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md) under "Way of working".
