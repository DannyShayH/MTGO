## Counting correction, 12 November 2026

For the next review: issue [#58](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/58) corrects work-item classification. Issues whose current titles begin with `Feature: ` are excluded even when empty. Previously they entered work-item counts until children were added. The generated charts and published JSON now recalculate the available history with Features excluded before counting, including cumulative flow and breach periods. Ordinary parents remain excluded, and eligible standalone and child work items retain their existing treatment.

Compare refreshed metrics using their displayed collection time; changed counts can reflect this correction rather than work delivered or removed. The provisional week 45 figures and observations above are preserved as originally written, not replaced with unverified totals. The correction does not recover renamed Features, archived or removed board evidence, or missing dates. Feature timing and the Features view remain separate work.
