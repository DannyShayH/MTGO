Every week the team holds a short flow review. The PL facilitates. The team reads the flow metrics, names old or blocked work items, and agrees on one improvement experiment for the next week.

Each review gets its own page here, named by year and ISO week (for example `2026-w46`), newest first. The template and the first review come with [issue #15](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/15).
