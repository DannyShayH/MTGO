Every week the team holds a short flow review. The PL facilitates. The team reads the flow metrics, names old or blocked work items, and agrees on one improvement experiment for the next week.

Each review gets its own page here, named by year and ISO week, newest first. Copy the [flow review template](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/blob/main/apps/docs/templates/flow-review.md) into this section, replace its placeholders, and use a negative `sidebar.order` based on the year and week. For example, `2026-w47` uses `-202647` and appears before `2026-w46`.

The [2026-w46 review](/MTGO/flow-reviews/2026-w46/) is being prepared. The team will record its observations and experiment after meeting.
