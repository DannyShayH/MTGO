A customer chooses food, pays with the order, follows its progress and rates the experience after delivery. The agreed MVP uses website ordering with mocked payment and SMS services, in Danish and English. Native apps, telephone ordering and scheduled orders are outside the MVP; see the [MVP boundary](/MTGO/product/mvp-and-roadmap/).

## Case requirements

- Customers have personal accounts. MTOGO supports Danish and English.
- Restaurants choose their own menus. MTOGO displays their offerings.
- Customers pay with the order through an external payment supplier. A mock of that service is permitted.
- There is no additional delivery charge; delivery is included in menu prices. Delivery agents do not handle payment.
- Customers receive SMS or app updates during preparation and delivery.
- After delivery, customers are asked to rate the food, overall experience and individual delivery agent.

## Proposed journey

**Actor:** Customer. **Goal:** Receive the selected food at the delivery address and understand what happens to the order.

## Exceptions to discuss

Use these scenarios to test the journey before splitting it into implementation issues.

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **A dish changes during checkout.** Its price or availability changes after
    it enters the basket. Recheck before payment and require review of the
    updated basket; do not silently substitute food or increase a charge.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Payment fails or times out.** Keep the customer informed and allow
    recovery without duplicate charges or Orders. An uncertain provider result
    stays pending while checked; an unresolved result after five minutes
    requires operator attention.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The Restaurant rejects the Order.** Rejection or five minutes without
    acceptance cancels it with a full refund owed. Failed refunds stay
    unresolved. Customers may cancel before acceptance; operator handling is
    required afterward, with no automatic change-of-mind refund once preparation
    starts.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Handover cannot be completed.** Contact attempts and operator escalation
    after ten minutes at the destination are required for an unreachable
    Customer. Never report a false delivery. Customer-caused failure has no
    automatic refund; MTOGO-caused failure requires a full refund.
  </li>
  <li>
    **An SMS cannot be delivered.** The website timeline remains available.
    Notification failure does not block food preparation or delivery; unresolved
    messages stay visible to operators and retries avoid duplicates.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [x] **Account flow:** Free browsing and sign-in before Order submission. Telephone ordering remains outside the MVP.
- [x] **Order scope:** One Restaurant location per Order, one Customer, one destination and one intended delivery. Ordered names, quantities, prices and the destination remain historical facts after later changes.
- [x] **Availability:** New Orders require an active, manually open Restaurant serving the postal-code destination and offering the selected items. Changes before payment require Customer review of the updated basket.
- [x] **Payment and acceptance:** Charge at submission and place the Order on success; Restaurant rejection or five-minute acceptance timeout cancels it with a full refund owed. Failed refunds stay visibly unresolved.
- [x] **Scheduling:** Immediate delivery in the MVP; scheduled orders are outside it.
- [x] **Feedback:** One voluntary, uneditable submission by the ordering Customer within seven days of delivery, with separate 1–5 food, experience and agent ratings.
- [x] **Updates:** The website timeline and mocked SMS cover placement, acceptance, preparation, readiness, pickup, delivery, cancellation and the Feedback invitation, in the Order language. Failed SMS does not block fulfilment and stays visible for operator attention.

Danish and English category and dish names are required; descriptions need both languages when supplied. Status messages use the language recorded with the Order. Complaint management is a future extension, outside the current project scope.
