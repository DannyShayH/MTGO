A customer chooses food, pays with the order, follows its progress and rates the experience after delivery. This journey focuses on website ordering; the case also describes app and telephone ordering channels. Their interfaces and inclusion in the first delivery need separate agreement.

## Case requirements

- Customers have personal accounts. MTOGO supports Danish and English.
- Restaurants choose their own menus. MTOGO displays their offerings.
- Customers pay with the order through an external payment supplier. A mock of that service is permitted.
- There is no additional delivery charge; delivery is included in menu prices. Drivers do not handle payment.
- Customers receive SMS or app updates during preparation and delivery.
- After delivery, customers are asked to rate the food, overall experience and individual driver.

## Proposed journey

**Actor:** Customer. **Goal:** Receive the selected food at the delivery address and understand what happens to the order.

## Exceptions to discuss

Use these scenarios to test the journey before splitting it into implementation issues.

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **A dish changes during checkout.** Its price or availability changes after
    it enters the basket. Decide what the customer must review before paying.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Payment fails or times out.** Keep the customer informed and allow
    recovery without duplicate charges or orders. Agree on how an uncertain
    provider result is resolved.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The restaurant cannot fulfil the order.** Decide how rejection or
    cancellation affects the payment and customer notification.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Delivery details cannot be used.** Decide how an invalid address or
    unreachable customer is handled and who takes the next action.
  </li>
  <li>
    **An update cannot be delivered.** Decide what happens when an SMS or app
    notification fails and where the customer can still check order status.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [ ] **Account flow:** When must the customer sign in or create an account? How does telephone ordering use that account?
- [ ] **Order scope:** Is one restaurant per order the agreed rule?
- [ ] **Availability:** Which opening-hours, service-area and sold-out checks prevent checkout?
- [ ] **Payment and acceptance:** When does the restaurant accept relative to payment, and how are rejection and refunds handled?
- [ ] **Scheduling:** Immediate orders only, or ordering for a later time?
- [ ] **Updates and feedback:** How is the notification channel chosen, what rating scale is used, and can feedback be edited?

The case requires Danish and English support; which menu fields and messages need both languages is still an implementation decision. Complaint management is a future extension, outside the current project scope.
