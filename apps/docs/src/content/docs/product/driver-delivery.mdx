## Case requirements and draft scope

**From the case:** MTOGO employs local delivery agents to collect food from restaurants and deliver it to customers. Customers pay with the order; drivers do not handle payment. Customers receive preparation and delivery updates through SMS or the app, then a feedback request that includes a rating for the individual driver.

The team must design a financially sustainable bonus scheme. The case suggests delivered order value, early or late hours and customer ratings as possible factors; it does not prescribe the calculation or amounts.

**Proposed interface:** A driver workspace for viewing delivery details and reporting progress. The case does not specify whether it is a web page or app, how drivers obtain deliveries, or whether they see ratings and bonus information. Those decisions remain open. Driver access does not imply general access to the internal MTOGO Medusa Admin.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** An authorized MTOGO delivery agent. **Starting point:** A restaurant order needs delivery. **Goal:** Collect the correct food, complete the handover and report accurate delivery progress.

## Connection to delivery states

The domain draft proposes the following connections. These state names are proposed business vocabulary, not a claim about implemented API values.

- **Taking responsibility:** The proposed lifecycle uses `PickupClaimed`. It currently assumes a driver claims a delivery before the restaurant begins preparation; both the assignment method and that ordering need team review.
- **Collecting food:** The restaurant indicates `ReadyForPickup`. The driver's pickup confirmation moves the delivery to `InTransit` in the proposed lifecycle.
- **Completing handover:** The driver's confirmation moves the delivery to `Delivered`, after which a feedback request is sent.

Signing in, viewing a destination and returning to the work list are driver actions; they do not require additional delivery states. Restaurant acceptance and preparation remain restaurant actions. The system sends customer notifications as progress is recorded.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **Two drivers try to claim the same delivery.** Agree on how one driver
    obtains responsibility and how the other is informed.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Food is not ready or the order cannot be found.** Define how the driver
    reports the problem, who resolves it and what the customer is told.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The driver can no longer complete the delivery.** Agree on reassignment
    before and after pickup, including responsibility for food already
    collected.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The address or handover fails.** Decide what happens when the customer is
    unreachable, the address is incorrect or delivery cannot be completed. Do
    not treat the delivery as successfully handed over without the agreed
    condition.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A status update fails or is repeated.** Preserve an accurate pickup and
    handover record when connectivity is lost or the driver retries an action.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Recorded information is disputed or corrected.** Agree on who can correct
    delivery events and how changes affect reporting and bonus calculations.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [ ] **Driver access:** Who creates driver accounts, which deliveries may a driver see, and when are customer contact details visible?
- [ ] **Assignment and availability:** Does the driver claim work or receive assignments? Can drivers decline or hold more than one delivery, and how is availability recorded?
- [ ] **Preparation sequence:** Must a driver take responsibility before the restaurant begins preparing food?
- [ ] **Handover and failure:** What confirms delivery, and how are unreachable customers, failed deliveries and reassignment handled?
- [ ] **Status corrections:** Who may correct pickup and handover events, and what history is retained?
- [ ] **Driver interface:** Web page or app? What delivery, contact and route information is needed for the first working increment?
- [ ] **Bonuses and ratings:** Which sustainable rules, time windows and rating scale apply? What, if anything, can drivers see about ratings, completed work and bonuses?

Live location tracking, route optimization, proof-of-delivery uploads and a driver earnings dashboard are not requirements specified by the case. Add them only if the team chooses and justifies them. Complaint management remains a future extension.
