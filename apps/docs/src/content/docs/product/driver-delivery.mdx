## Case requirements and agreed scope

**From the case:** MTOGO employs local delivery agents to collect food from restaurants and deliver it to customers. Customers pay with the order; delivery agents do not handle payment. Customers receive preparation and delivery updates through SMS or the app, then a feedback request that includes a rating for the individual delivery agent.

The team must design a financially sustainable bonus scheme. The case suggests delivered order value, early or late hours and customer ratings as possible factors; it does not prescribe the calculation or amounts.

**Proposed interface:** A delivery agent workspace for viewing delivery details and reporting progress. The case does not specify whether it is a web page or app, how delivery agents obtain deliveries, or whether they see ratings and bonus information. The MVP uses a website; area-based claiming and the core access boundary are agreed, and agents can see their own completed work, points and provisional/final awards. Delivery agent access does not imply general access to the internal MTOGO Medusa Admin.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** An authorized MTOGO delivery agent. **Starting point:** A restaurant order needs delivery. **Goal:** Collect the correct food, complete the handover and report accurate delivery progress.

## Connection to delivery states

The agreed business lifecycle has the following connections. These describe business progress; concrete API values are decided during the Feature grill.

- **Taking responsibility:** Agents claim accepted delivery work in their operating area, holding at most one active delivery. Preparation proceeds independently after acceptance; pickup requires both ready food and an assigned agent.
- **Collecting food:** The restaurant indicates `ReadyForPickup`. The delivery agent's pickup confirmation moves the delivery to `InTransit` in the proposed lifecycle.
- **Completing handover:** The delivery agent's confirmation moves the delivery to `Delivered`, after which a feedback request is sent.

Signing in, viewing a destination and returning to the work list are delivery agent actions; they do not require additional delivery states. Restaurant acceptance and preparation remain restaurant actions. The system sends customer notifications as progress is recorded.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **Two delivery agents try to claim the same delivery.** Only one obtains
    responsibility. The losing agent must not appear assigned; detailed
    interaction design belongs to the Feature grill.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Food is not ready or the order cannot be found.** Define how the delivery
    agent reports the problem, who resolves it and what the customer is told.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The delivery agent can no longer complete the delivery.** Escalate to an
    MTOGO operator. Agent release and reassignment are outside the MVP, before
    and after pickup. The workflow must not silently transfer responsibility.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The handover fails.** Confirm delivery only after handing food to the
    Customer or designated recipient. Contact attempts and operator escalation
    after ten minutes at the destination are required for an unreachable
    Customer. Customer-caused failure has no automatic refund; MTOGO-caused
    failure requires a full refund.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A status update fails or is repeated.** Preserve an accurate pickup and
    handover record when connectivity is lost or the delivery agent retries an
    action.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Recorded information is corrected.** An operator records the reason and
    retains original events and who made the change. Financial consequences of
    corrections must be reconciled rather than silently rewriting history.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [x] **Delivery agent access:** Operators create access. Agents see available work in their area and assigned deliveries; Customer contact details become visible after assignment.
- [x] **Assignment:** Agents claim available work in their operating area, with at most one active delivery and one responsible agent per delivery; operators can intervene.
- [x] **Unclaimed work:** Alert an operator after ten minutes and cancel after twenty if still unassigned, from acceptance. MTOGO bears prepared-food loss and the Customer receives a full refund.
- [x] **Release and reassignment:** Outside the MVP, before and after pickup.
- [x] **Stalled assigned work and operating areas:** Operators manage areas. Attention is required after thirty minutes from acceptance without pickup or thirty minutes from pickup without delivery; operators record continuation or failure.
- [x] **Preparation sequence:** Preparation and assignment proceed independently after acceptance. Pickup requires ready food and an assigned agent.
- [x] **Handover and failure:** Handover to the Customer or designated recipient; contact attempts and operator escalation after ten minutes for an unreachable Customer. Customer-caused failure has no automatic refund; MTOGO-caused failure requires a full refund.
- [x] **Status corrections:** Operators may correct mistakes with a reason, retaining original events and the identity of the person making the correction.
- [x] **Delivery agent journey:** Website access to eligible work, assigned pickup/destination/contact details and own work/bonus history. Detailed presentation and navigation interactions are refined in the Feature grill.
- [x] **Bonus funding and rating subjects:** The monthly pool is capped at 20% of commission after refunds/reversals, excluding service fees. Only the separate 1–5 agent rating may influence the individual rating-based bonus.
- [x] **Bonus points and finalization:** Delivered Order value excluding VAT, multiplied by 1.25 for early/late pickup and 1.10 for agent ratings of 4 or 5. Share the pool proportionally and finalize on the 8th; later refunds affect future funding without wage deductions.
- [x] **Bonus financial details and visibility:** Approved refund obligations, food compensation and carried losses reduce funding; full pre-finalization refunds remove points. Awards round down to DKK 0.01 increments. Agents see their own completed work, points and clearly distinguished provisional/final awards.

Live location tracking, route optimization, proof-of-delivery uploads and a delivery agent earnings dashboard are not requirements specified by the case. Add them only if the team chooses and justifies them. Complaint management remains a future extension.
