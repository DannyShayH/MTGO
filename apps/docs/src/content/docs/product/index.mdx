MTOGO connects customers, restaurants and employed delivery agents. These drafts help the team and teacher discuss how ordering, restaurant content, delivery work and internal setup fit together.

:::note[Drafts for shared understanding]
The workflows use the supplied MTOGO case, the domain-model draft and the team's choice of Medusa Admin for internal restaurant setup. They separate case requirements from proposed behavior. Open questions remain for the team; these pages do not claim that the workflows are implemented or approved.
:::

## Review together

For each workflow, walk through the normal path, then test the exceptions. Confirm the actor's permissions, the information they need and the result of each action. Record agreed answers or follow-up requirements in [issue #10](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/10).

The case permits a coherent working subset of the product. Describing a workflow here does not commit every step or channel to the MVP. The MVP boundary, roadmap and remaining user journeys still need agreement. The prioritised [project board](https://github.com/users/DannyShayH/projects/2) tracks the work selected for implementation.
