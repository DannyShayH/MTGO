MTOGO connects customers, restaurants and employed delivery agents. These documents record how ordering, Restaurant content, delivery work and internal setup fit together, with implementation refined per Feature.

:::note[Business model agreed on 11 November 2026]
The team has agreed the business rules, shared language, MVP boundary, evolving Feature backlog and provisional supervision milestones. The glossary and ADRs record decisions; explicitly parked optional questions have reasons. Detailed interactions and implementation are refined in later Feature grills. These documents do not claim implemented functionality.
:::

## Refine one Feature at a time

When a Feature is pulled, walk through its user stories and exceptions with an agent, preserving the shared business rules. The team then calls `/to-spec` for that existing Feature parent and `/to-tickets` for its vertical work-item sub-issues. New issues remain in Backlog; humans move work to Ready and start it.

The case permits a coherent working subset of the product. Describing a workflow here does not commit every step or channel to the MVP. The [MVP boundary and provisional demo goals](/MTGO/product/mvp-and-roadmap/) are agreed; the [Feature backlog](/MTGO/product/feature-backlog-proposal/) records agreed MoSCoW scope, stories and native blockers. The prioritised [project board](https://github.com/users/DannyShayH/projects/2) tracks the work selected for implementation.
