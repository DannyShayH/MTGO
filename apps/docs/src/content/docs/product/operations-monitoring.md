Discovery [#257](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/257) refines [Feature #54](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/54). This page records discovery in progress rather than a specification or implemented capability. The team invokes `/to-spec` and `/to-tickets` separately.

Earlier decisions stand. [ADR-0015](/MTGO/architecture/decisions/0015-management-measures-and-visible-exclusions/) defines the management measures; this discovery extends it with some of the teacher's suggested business metrics and records where it differs.

## Refined user stories

- As an MTOGO operator, I want one Operations page showing Open Orders by stage, unassigned deliveries, deliveries needing attention and unresolved Full refunds right now, so I can see where work is stuck.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want last-24-hour placements, Orders per hour and placements per day for the previous seven days, so I can see volume and peaks.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want Order processing time, Delivery transit time and the Unfulfilled Order rate by cause, each with its period, population and exclusions, so I can judge speed and reliability without misreading incomplete data.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want to narrow the page to one Restaurant, so I can look into a single kitchen.
- As Restaurant staff, I want the same volume, processing and unfulfilled measures for my own Restaurant in Danish or English, so I understand my own service.
- As a team, we want a realistic demo world on staging, with Scarpetta as the prime Restaurant, so #51, #52, #53 and #54 can be demonstrated on coherent, living data.

## Facts found in the code

- An Order exists only after successful payment; its `OrderOperation` record is the paid-placement fact. Legacy Orders placed before that record existed have no `placed_at`.
- Operational states are `awaiting_acceptance`, `accepted`, `preparing`, `ready` and `cancelled`. Pickup and handover live on `IntendedDelivery`. An ended Order has a delivery outcome of `delivered` or `not_delivered`; `delivery_ended_at` copies the delivery's terminal time.
- Cancellation categories are `restaurant_rejected`, `acceptance_timeout`, `customer_cancelled`, `unassigned_timeout` and `operator_cancelled`.
- Attention types are `unassigned`, `access_revoked`, `assigned_without_pickup`, `in_transit_without_delivery`, `arrived_without_handover` and `agent_reported_problem`.
- Full refunds have the status `pending`, `failed` or `succeeded`.
- Uncertain and delayed payment results are not recorded: #134 and #135 were closed as not planned, and the mock payment resolves immediately.
- No promised delivery time is recorded. Estimated arrival times are Feature #56 (Could have).
- Neither Medusa Admin nor the Restaurant-Delivery Marketplace recipe provides analytics or chart components.

### Measure definitions

| Question                      | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q5: Open Orders               | An Open Order is an Order that is not cancelled and has no delivery outcome. Show the total and a breakdown by stage: awaiting acceptance; accepted or preparing; ready but unassigned; assigned, not yet picked up; on the way. Legacy Orders without `placed_at` still count, because being open does not depend on a time.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Q6: Unresolved payments       | Because uncertain payments are not recorded, ADR-0015's "unresolved payments/refunds" becomes **unresolved Full refunds**, with `pending` and `failed` shown separately. ADR-0015 is amended to say uncertain payments are not tracked in the MVP.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Q7: Cancellations in 24 hours | The cancellation figure counts Orders placed in the last 24 hours that are now cancelled, the same population as placements, split by cancellation category. A cancelled Order still counts in the volume.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Q8: Order processing time     | From `placed_at` to handover, for Orders delivered in the last 24 hours, selected by handover time. Shown as an average with the number of Orders included and the exclusions with reasons (no `placed_at`, no handover time). Cancelled and not-delivered Orders never count. No median for now.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Q9: Unfulfilled Orders (%)    | An **Unfulfilled Order** ended without its food being handed over: any cancellation category, or a delivery that ended `not_delivered`. The rate is unfulfilled ÷ ended among Orders placed in the last 24 hours. Orders still open are in neither number and are shown as "still open", so recent Orders cannot flatter the rate. The figure is split by cause, so Customer cancellations stay visible apart from Restaurant and MTOGO causes. "Failed" stays the name of a delivery outcome. |
| Q10: Delivery transit time    | From recorded pickup (`picked_up_at`) to handover, for Orders delivered in the last 24 hours, selected by handover time. Orders without `picked_up_at` are shown as exclusions. "Delivery time" is avoided because Customers read it as an arrival time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Q11: Orders per hour          | Placements in each of the last 24 Copenhagen clock hours. The current hour is labelled "so far". An empty hour shows 0, because no Orders is a real zero rather than missing data. On the night the clocks go back, the repeated hour appears twice, labelled with its UTC offset.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Q13: Unassigned and attention | **Unassigned deliveries** counts Open Orders whose delivery has no agent yet, with the number waiting over ten minutes beside it. **Deliveries needing attention** counts outstanding attention items by type; unassigned attention items are marked as the same Orders, so the overlap is explicit.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Q14: Links                    | The attention and Full refund figures link to the existing Admin `deliveries` and `refunds` pages, which own those lists. The Operations page shows counts only, never a second list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Q15: Customer rating          | The Operations page shows #51's platform-wide experience rating for its default last 30 days (average and number of ratings), labelled with that period and linking to the Feedback page. It is hidden whenever the `feedback` flag is off.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Q16: Seven-day average        | Platform placements over the previous seven complete Copenhagen calendar days divided by seven. A 23- or 25-hour day counts as one day, and zero-Order days count. This is ADR-0015 unchanged; partial Restaurant activity is decided with the staff view.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

### Delivery

| Question                  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q4: Calculation           | Measures are calculated in the database on each read. Each module service runs one count or average query over a fixed period, and one read workflow combines them. Rows are never loaded to calculate in Node, modules are not joined in SQL, and indexes on `placed_at` and `delivery_ended_at` are added and confirmed with the query plan. The page shows an "as of" time and a refresh button; there are no stored snapshots and no live push. No ADR: the choice is easy to change behind the read workflow.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Q12: Charts               | Four measures are charts: Orders per hour (24 bars); placements per day for the previous seven days, with the seven-day average drawn as a line; Open Orders by stage; and Unfulfilled Orders by cause. Processing time, transit time, the rating, Full refunds and unassigned deliveries stay figure cards. Every chart states its period and population, keeps its numbers available as a table for screen readers and never relies on colour alone. Charts use Recharts with Medusa UI colours, in the Admin Operations page first; the later staff view reuses the same chart components. The library must support both the Admin's React 18 and the partner app's React 19. |
| Q17: Feature flag         | The Operations page and its API sit behind a new default-off flag, `operations-monitoring` (ADR-0022).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Q21: One moment per read  | Each read fixes one "as of" time and calculates every period relative to it. Each total and its breakdown (Open Orders by stage, Unfulfilled Orders by cause) come from the same query, so they always add up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Q23: Recording the change | ADR-0015 gets a dated revision section for the added measures, the narrowing to unresolved Full refunds and leaving out on-time deliveries, rather than a new ADR.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

### Scaling path

Every measure covers a fixed recent period, so each read touches only recent rows: about 550 Orders for 24 hours and 3,800 for seven days at the case's 200,000 Orders a year, however much history accumulates. Climb only with evidence:

1. Calculate in the database on read (chosen).
2. Cache the combined result for about 30 seconds per scope when many people watch the page at once.
3. Keep hourly totals, updated when an Order changes, when long-range trends or much larger volumes are added.
4. Use a read replica or a separate analytics store at much larger scale.

## Medusa and recipe comparison

- Neither the Restaurant-Delivery Marketplace recipe nor Medusa Eats includes reporting or statistics; their dashboards are live delivery cards, which #49 already used.
- Medusa Admin is extended with a UI route (`defineRouteConfig`, a sidebar label and icon) and optionally widgets. `@medusajs/ui` and the `@medusajs/dashboard` exports offer containers and data tables but no charts, so Recharts is added (Q12).
- Medusa's query graph lists and filters records but does not aggregate. Counts and averages therefore run as module service methods, following the existing `@InjectManager` pattern in `order-operation`, combined in one read workflow (Q4).
- The partner app reads through its own authenticated `/partner/*` routes, as statements and ratings already do.

## Boundaries

- **#46 paid ordering:** only successful payment creates an Order; uncertain payments are not tracked (#134 and #135 closed as not planned).
- **#47 acceptance and preparation, #48 claiming and delivering:** own the recorded times and outcomes; #54 only reads them.
- **#49 operator attention and refunds:** owns the attention and refund lists; #54 shows counts with the same definitions and links to them.
- **#51 Feedback:** owns the rating calculation; #54 shows its platform-wide figure.
- **#52 statements and #53 agent bonuses:** own all money figures. #54 adds no revenue or financial measure and no missed-finalization alert. Both benefit from the demo world.
- **#56 estimated arrival times:** a prerequisite for any on-time measure.
- **#167 Prometheus monitoring:** owns technical latency; Order processing time is a business measure and stays in #54.

## Out of scope

Agreed in Q24: on-time deliveries (%) until #56; revenue and money figures, which belong to #52 and #53; top dishes, comparing Restaurants with each other and trends longer than seven days; alerts or notifications, including #53's missed-finalization alert; exports; uncertain-payment tracking; and live push updates.
