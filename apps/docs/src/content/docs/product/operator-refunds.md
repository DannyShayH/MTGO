Discovery [#196](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/196) for [Feature #49](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/49) was confirmed by the team on 22 November 2026, including explicit confirmation that a shared understanding was reached. The team deliberately kept the Feature small: operators can see and retry failed full refunds, cancel an accepted Order with a full refund, and see problems that Delivery agents report. Specification, ticket splitting and implementation follow separately.

## Refined user stories

- As an MTOGO operator, I want to see every Order whose full refund is pending or failed and retry a failed one, so no Customer is left unpaid and none is paid twice.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want to cancel an accepted, unfinished Order with a full refund and a reason, so I can resolve failures that the automatic rules do not cover.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want to see who triggered each refund attempt, when and with what outcome, so refunds stay explainable.
- As a Delivery agent, I want to report a problem with my active delivery, so an operator knows to look at it.

## Source evidence

- Automatic cancellations (Restaurant rejection, acceptance timeout, Customer cancellation before acceptance and thirty-minute unassigned cancellation) already create a `RefundObligation` and attempt the refund through `attemptFullRefund` in `apps/backend/src/workflows/order-operations/full-refund.ts`. Each attempt reuses the stored amount and idempotency key.
- The recovery job retries `pending` obligations only. A `failed` obligation is left owed for the operator, with no operator view or action yet (`steps/recover-order-operations.ts`).
- The obligation records `attempt_count` and `last_attempted_at`, but not who triggered an attempt. A failed attempt leaves no Medusa refund row.
- `OrderOperation` already has `cancelled_by`, `cancellation_category` and `cancellation_note`; no operator category exists yet.
- `endDeliveryForOrder` (`apps/backend/src/workflows/delivery/terminal.ts`) ends a delivery as `cancelled` and resolves its attention; #162 uses it together with the MTOGO-funded prepared-food credit.
- `DeliveryAttention` holds one record per Order and type and ends with the Order; `access_revoked` shows the pattern of attention raised at once. Operators see it in the Admin `deliveries` route.

## Agreed decisions

| Question                  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Q1: Failed refunds        | A Medusa Admin Refunds page lists Orders with a pending or failed full refund: amount, failure reason, attempts and history. **Retry refund** is available for failed refunds; pending ones are shown read-only. A retry reuses the stored amount and idempotency key. A refund is resolved only on provider success.                      |
| Q2: Operator cancellation | One action, **Cancel with full refund**, for accepted, unfinished Orders (accepted, preparing, ready, assigned or picked up), with a required free-text reason. Always a full refund; no partial refund and no cancellation without refund. A Customer-caused failed handover is handled by not cancelling.                                |
| Q3: Reduced scope         | Out of scope for #49: a combined attention view, corrections of recorded mistakes, goodwill refunds and who bears the loss, the five-minute uncertain-payment condition, and reducing the Bonus pool for unresolved refunds (#53 reads refund records when built). Q10–Q12 later brought the problem report back in a minimal form.        |
| Q4: Where                 | Medusa Admin, English only (ADR-0018). Nothing operator-facing in the partner app.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Q5: Refund history        | Every attempt records its time, who triggered it (operator or "System") and the outcome. Each attempt also writes one safe log line with the Order and actor only (ADR-0019).                                                                                                                                                              |
| Q6: Who cancelled         | Operator cancellation records `cancelled_by` (the operator), a new category `operator_cancelled` and the reason as `cancellation_note`. Admin shows the operator by name.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Q7: Delivery in progress  | Cancelling an assigned or picked-up Order ends its delivery as `cancelled` through the existing terminal step: the agent is free for new work and open attention is resolved. No reassignment.                                                                                                                                             |
| Q8: Prepared food         | When preparation had started, the Restaurant receives the MTOGO-funded prepared-food credit, as in #162. No responsibility selector.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Q9: Roles                 | Any MTOGO operator may retry and cancel. Restaurant staff and Delivery agents are refused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q10: Problem report       | A single **Report a problem** action in the agent's workspace while they hold an assigned or picked-up delivery. It raises `agent_reported_problem` attention at once in the existing attention list and changes nothing about the delivery.                                                                                               |
| Q11: Report contents      | A fixed category (Restaurant closed / can't collect, Food missing or wrong, Can't find the address, Other) and an optional note of at most 280 characters. Danish and English labels in the partner app; English in Admin. Customer unreachable stays with arrival and contact attempts (#164). The note never goes into application logs. |
| Q12: Repeats and ending   | At most one problem report per delivery; a repeat shows "Already reported" and keeps the first. It leaves the attention list when the Order ends. No operator acknowledge or resolve action.                                                                                                                                               |

[ADR-0011](/MTGO/architecture/decisions/0011-order-failures-cancellation-and-mvp-handover/) records the reduced scope. The [glossary](/MTGO/architecture/glossary/) defines Full refund, Operator cancellation and Problem report.

## Acceptance examples

| Scenario                                                                                              | Expected result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The refund for a thirty-minute unassigned cancellation fails at the provider                          | The Order appears on the Refunds page as failed with its reason. An operator retries and it succeeds. The history shows the System attempt and the operator's retry; the Customer is paid once.                                                                          |
| An operator presses Retry twice in quick succession                                                   | The provider receives one refund; the second press finds it in progress or succeeded.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A refund is still pending                                                                             | It is listed without a Retry action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| An operator cancels a picked-up Order with the reason "Agent's bike broke down"                       | The Order is cancelled with the operator, `operator_cancelled` and the reason recorded; a full refund is attempted; the delivery ends `cancelled`, the agent is free and open attention is resolved; a prepared-food credit is recorded because preparation had started. |
| An operator cancels an accepted Order before preparation started                                      | Full refund attempted; no prepared-food credit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| An operator tries to cancel without a reason, or cancels a delivered Order or one awaiting acceptance | Refused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Restaurant staff or a Delivery agent calls an operator refund or cancellation endpoint                | Refused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| An agent reports "Food missing or wrong" and taps again                                               | One `agent_reported_problem` item with the category and note; the second tap shows "Already reported"; the delivery is unchanged.                                                                                                                                        |
| An agent without an active delivery tries to report a problem                                         | Refused; the action is not shown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| An Order with a problem report is delivered or cancelled                                              | The report leaves the attention list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Delivered so far

- [#197](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/197): every real Full refund attempt is kept in an append-only history with its time, who triggered it and its outcome (`succeeded`, `failed` with its safe failure code, or `in_progress`). Failed attempts stay in the history although the provider leaves no refund row. Restaurant rejection, Customer cancellation and their timeout outcomes record the cancellation's actor; the recovery sweep and the thirty-minute unassigned cancellation record `system`. A repeat or sweep that leaves the refund untouched adds nothing. Each attempt also writes one `refund.attempted` log record with only the Order, actor and outcome class. Operators see the history on the Refunds page (#198).
- [#198](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/198): a **Refunds** page in Medusa Admin, next to Deliveries, lists every Order whose Full refund is failed or pending: Order reference (linking to the Order), Restaurant, amount, status, the safe failure reason, attempts, last attempt, how and by whom the Order was cancelled with the reason, and an expandable attempt history ("System" or the operator's or staff member's name). Failed refunds come first, then pending ones, longest owed first; the page is paginated. **Retry refund** appears only on failed refunds. A retry runs under the Order's settlement lock through the one existing refund attempt, with the stored amount and idempotency key: a refund the provider already made is recorded without contacting it, money refunded another way is never sent on top, and the operator is recorded as the attempt's actor. The page shows the outcome at once; a succeeded refund leaves the page, a failed one stays with its reason. Pressing Retry twice, or two operators at once, moves the money once: the later request finds the refund succeeded and shows it. A retry of a refund still in progress is refused with `refund_not_failed`. Only operators can list and retry. Endpoints: `GET /admin/refunds` and `POST /admin/refunds/{order_id}/retry` in the [API documentation](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/blob/main/apps/backend/src/api/README.md). The HTTP suite `operator-refunds.spec.ts` proves the money rules against the mocked provider. The disposable browser journey in the operator suite shows a real failed refund with its reason, cancellation and attempt history, retries it and sees it succeed and leave the page. To see a failed refund locally, run `npx medusa exec ./src/scripts/seed-demo-failed-refund.ts` from `apps/backend` against a development or disposable database: it creates its own Restaurant and Customer and leaves one Order whose refund failed at the provider, while the running backend's provider keeps working, so Retry succeeds.

- [#199](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/199): **Cancel with full refund** in Medusa Admin, on each item in the Deliveries page's attention list, on each unclaimed delivery and on the Order details page, which covers an assigned or picked-up Order with no attention. The confirmation shows the amount the Customer gets back and requires a reason. The server allows it for accepted, preparing and ready Orders, also with an assigned or picked-up delivery, and refuses it before acceptance (`order_not_accepted`) and after handover (`order_delivered`). Under the Order's guard, the one that claim, pickup and handover take, the cancellation is recorded with the operator, `operator_cancelled` and the reason, together with the Full refund obligation and, when preparation had started, the MTOGO-funded prepared-food credit. The delivery ends as `cancelled` with its milestones kept, the agent is free for new work and the Order's attention is resolved. The refund is then attempted as the operator's; a provider failure leaves it on the Refunds page and the Order stays cancelled. A repeat or a double click returns the recorded outcome and refunds once. The Customer's timeline says MTOGO cancelled the Order and shows the refund. Restaurant staff see "Cancelled by MTOGO" without the operator's reason. For thirty minutes, the agent's workspace says MTOGO cancelled the delivery and that they can claim new work. Endpoints: `GET /admin/orders/{id}/operator-cancel` for the amount the confirmation shows and `POST /admin/orders/{id}/operator-cancel` to cancel, in the [API documentation](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/blob/main/apps/backend/src/api/README.md). The HTTP suite `operator-cancellation.spec.ts` covers every allowed stage, the refusals, repeats, a failing provider, operator-only access and races with claim, pickup and handover.

## Problem report implementation (#200)

The agent's current Delivery card offers a Danish or English Report a problem
form. It accepts one of four fixed categories and an optional 280-character
note. The authenticated partner endpoint holds the agent and Order guards and
stores the first report as immediately raised Delivery attention. A repeat
returns that first report with “Already reported.” The Admin Deliveries
attention list shows the category, note, reporter and age. Reporting leaves
the Delivery unchanged; terminal completion resolves the attention. The note
is absent from application logs.

## Evidence needed in implementation

- HTTP tests for operator-only access, the refused states and repeated or concurrent retries and cancellations, including a cancellation racing an agent's pickup or handover.
- A retry after a lost provider response that still pays once.
- Refund history entries for System and operator attempts, and log records without provider text or the note.
- The problem report's one-per-delivery rule under double taps and retries, and its Danish and English labels.
