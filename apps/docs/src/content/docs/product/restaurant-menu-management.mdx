## Case requirements and agreed scope

**From the case:** Each restaurant chooses its own menu. Customers can view it on the website or app. Menu prices include delivery; customers pay no additional delivery charge. MTOGO supports Danish and English.

**From the agreed domain model:** RestaurantStaff maintain categories and dishes, including prices, descriptions, allergens and sold-out status. The team has agreed bilingual names, descriptions in both languages when supplied, Restaurant-provided allergen information with missing information visibly unknown, positive DKK prices including VAT and delivery, and immediately published valid changes. The restaurant workspace is distinct from the internal MTOGO Medusa Admin workflow; the case does not prescribe its interface.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** Authorized restaurant staff. **Starting point:** The restaurant and its staff access have been set up. **Goal:** Customers see an accurate menu that the restaurant can fulfil.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **Missing or invalid information.** Explain which field needs attention and
    preserve the staff member's edits.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A dish is sold out while a Customer checks out.** Recheck availability
    before payment and require review of the updated basket; no silent
    substitutions.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A dish price changes.** Historical Order names, quantities and prices
    remain unchanged. A price change before payment requires Customer review of
    the updated basket, never a silently increased charge.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Two staff members edit the same dish.** Decide how conflicting changes are
    detected or resolved.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A staff member tries to edit another restaurant.** Reject the action and
    keep restaurant ownership clear throughout the workflow.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A dish or category is removed.** Decide what happens to its existing
    orders, active baskets and remaining menu items.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [x] **Access:** Operators manage Restaurant staff access; authorized staff manage and publish only their own Restaurant menu.
- [x] **Required content:** Danish and English category and dish names; optional descriptions in both languages when supplied. Restaurants supply allergens; missing information stays visibly unknown.
- [x] **Price boundary:** Positive DKK amounts including VAT and delivery.
- [x] **Financial detail:** Exercise menu VAT is 25%; commission uses the food value excluding VAT and is rounded once to two decimals.
- [x] **Publishing:** Valid saved changes become visible immediately, without MTOGO dish approval.
- [x] **Availability:** Sold-out dishes remain visible but unavailable. Ordering requires an active, manually open Restaurant serving the destination.
- [x] **Historical Orders:** Preserve ordered names, quantities and prices after price changes or deletion; checkout changes require Customer review.
- [ ] **Concurrent staff edits:** Agree the user-visible conflict behavior during the Feature grill.

The restaurant owns the menu decisions. Any proposed MTOGO approval or restriction needs an explicit reason and team agreement; it is not specified by the case.
