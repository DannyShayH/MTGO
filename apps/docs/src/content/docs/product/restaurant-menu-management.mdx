## Case requirements and draft scope

**From the case:** Each restaurant chooses its own menu. Customers can view it on the website or app. Menu prices include delivery; customers pay no additional delivery charge. MTOGO supports Danish and English.

**From the domain draft:** RestaurantStaff maintain categories and dishes, including prices, descriptions, allergens and sold-out status. These fields and the restaurant workspace below are proposals. The restaurant workspace is distinct from the internal MTOGO Medusa Admin workflow; the case does not prescribe its interface.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** Authorized restaurant staff. **Starting point:** The restaurant and its staff access have been set up. **Goal:** Customers see an accurate menu that the restaurant can fulfil.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **Missing or invalid information.** Explain which field needs attention and
    preserve the staff member's edits.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A dish is sold out while a customer checks out.** Agree on the final
    availability check and the message shown to the customer.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The price of an ordered dish changes.** Agree that historical order
    information remains understandable and define how in-progress baskets are
    treated.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Two staff members edit the same dish.** Decide how conflicting changes are
    detected or resolved.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A staff member tries to edit another restaurant.** Reject the action and
    keep restaurant ownership clear throughout the workflow.
  </li>
  <li>
    **A dish or category is removed.** Decide what happens to its existing
    orders, active baskets and remaining menu items.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [ ] **Access:** Who can invite restaurant staff, and what roles may edit or publish menu content?
- [ ] **Required content:** Which dish fields are mandatory, and which need Danish and English versions?
- [ ] **Price rules:** What currency, VAT handling, rounding and valid price range apply?
- [ ] **Publishing:** Are saved changes immediately visible, or is there a draft/publish step? Is any review needed?
- [ ] **Availability:** Does sold-out status hide a dish or keep it visible but unavailable? How do opening hours affect ordering?
- [ ] **Change history:** How are price changes, deletion and simultaneous edits handled without losing order history?

The restaurant owns the menu decisions. Any proposed MTOGO approval or restriction needs an explicit reason and team agreement; it is not specified by the case.
