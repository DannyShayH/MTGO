## Case requirements and team direction

**From the case:** Restaurants supply the food and choose their own menus. They pay MTOGO a service fee and commission on order value excluding VAT. The commission scale and bonus scheme are not fully defined; the team must choose sustainable rules.

**Team direction:** Internal MTOGO staff use Medusa Admin to add restaurants. The case does not specify the onboarding screens, required fields, staff invitations or the conditions for accepting orders.

**Boundary:** An MTOGO internal user and a restaurant staff user are different roles in the domain draft. Setting up restaurant staff access does not imply giving those users general access to Medusa Admin.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** Authorized MTOGO employee using Medusa Admin. **Goal:** A correctly identified restaurant can maintain its menu and, once ready, receive customer orders.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **A restaurant already exists.** Confirm whether this is the same business,
    a separate branch or a duplicate before creating another record.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Setup is incomplete.** Decide whether a partial record can be saved and
    how staff can see what remains before orders are accepted.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Staff access cannot be completed.** Decide how invitations are retried,
    corrected or revoked without exposing another restaurant's information.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Commercial terms are unresolved.** Decide what blocks readiness until the
    team and restaurant have agreed on the applicable fees.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The restaurant stops operating.** Decide how it stops receiving new orders
    while existing orders, financial records and settlements remain accessible.
  </li>
  <li>
    **An unauthorized user attempts a change.** Enforce the agreed internal and
    restaurant-specific access boundaries.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [ ] **Internal permissions:** Which MTOGO roles may add, edit or deactivate restaurants?
- [ ] **Restaurant identity:** Which fields are mandatory, what identifies a duplicate, and how are multiple branches represented?
- [ ] **Commercial terms:** What are the service fee and commission calculation, and are they shared or restaurant-specific?
- [ ] **Staff access:** Who invites restaurant users, what roles do they have, and how is access removed?
- [ ] **Readiness:** Is there an explicit draft/active state or approval gate, and what must be true before orders are accepted?
- [ ] **Deactivation:** Who may stop new orders, and how are active orders and historical records handled?

The case also asks for a management dashboard of order status and processing measures. Its daily operational workflow is separate from onboarding and is not specified by this draft. Complaint management remains a future extension.
