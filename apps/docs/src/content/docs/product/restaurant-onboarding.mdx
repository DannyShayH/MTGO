## Case requirements and team direction

**From the case:** Restaurants supply the food and choose their own menus. They pay MTOGO a service fee and commission on order value excluding VAT. Marginal commission tiers, a DKK 100 excluding VAT monthly service fee and a commission-funded bonus cap are agreed; billing, bonus allocation and food-credit responsibility are recorded in the shared domain rules.

**Team direction:** Internal MTOGO staff use Medusa Admin to add restaurants. The case does not specify the onboarding screens, required fields, staff invitations or the conditions for accepting orders.

**Boundary:** MTOGO operator and Restaurant staff are agreed, distinct business roles. Setting up restaurant staff access does not imply giving those users general access to Medusa Admin.

## Proposed workflow

**Actor:** Authorized MTOGO employee using Medusa Admin. **Goal:** A correctly identified restaurant can maintain its menu and, once ready, receive customer orders.

## Exceptions to discuss

<ul class="review-criteria">
  <li>
    **A restaurant already exists.** Confirm whether this is the same business,
    a separate branch or a duplicate before creating another record.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Setup is incomplete.** Decide whether a partial record can be saved and
    how staff can see what remains before orders are accepted.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Staff access cannot be completed.** Decide how invitations are retried,
    corrected or revoked without exposing another restaurant's information.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Commercial terms are unresolved.** Decide what blocks readiness until the
    team and restaurant have agreed on the applicable fees.
  </li>
  <li>
    **The restaurant stops operating.** Decide how it stops receiving new orders
    while existing orders, financial records and settlements remain accessible.
  </li>
  <li>
    **An unauthorized user attempts a change.** Enforce the agreed internal and
    restaurant-specific access boundaries.
  </li>
</ul>

## Open decisions

- [x] **Creation and activation:** MTOGO operators create and explicitly activate Restaurants.
- [x] **Access administration:** Operators manage Restaurant staff and agent access, operating areas, activation/deactivation and commercial terms.
- [x] **Restaurant identity:** One preparation/pickup location per Restaurant, with separate branches represented separately. Activation requires address, contacts, staff, commercial terms, menu and delivery area; extra form and duplicate-detection interactions belong to the Feature grill.
- [x] **Commercial terms:** Marginal commission tiers and a fixed monthly service fee per active Restaurant are agreed. The common MVP service fee is DKK 100 excluding VAT per active Restaurant per month. Prorate by active calendar days, including temporary closure, and preserve historical amounts when terms change.
- [x] **Staff access:** Operators manage access. Restaurant staff manage only their own menus, opening, acceptance and preparation; revocation preserves history.
- [x] **Readiness:** Explicit operator activation requires a pickup address, contact details, authorized staff, agreed terms, a published menu and postal-code delivery area.
- [x] **Opening:** Active/inactive participation is separate from manual open/closed availability. Closing or deactivating a Restaurant does not abandon existing Orders.
- [x] **Deactivation:** Operators control active/inactive participation; Restaurant staff control manual opening. Stopping new Orders does not abandon existing Orders or remove history.

The case also asks for a management dashboard of order status and processing measures. Its daily operational workflow is separate from onboarding and is covered by the agreed management measures in the domain model. Complaint management remains a future extension.
