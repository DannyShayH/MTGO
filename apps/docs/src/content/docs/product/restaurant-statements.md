Discovery [#223](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/223) refines [Feature #52](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/issues/52). The team accepted Q1–Q19 on 24 November 2026, with Q19 explicitly revised to 100 synthetic Customers and 500 Orders, and then confirmed shared understanding. Discovery is agreed. This page records discovery rather than a specification or implemented capability. The team invokes `/to-spec` and `/to-tickets` separately.

## Refined user stories

- As Restaurant staff, I want to select a month and inspect my Restaurant's food credits, commission, service fee, adjustments and monthly balance with their evidence, so I can understand that month's commercial activity.
- As Restaurant staff, I want closed statements to stay fixed and later adjustments to explain their original month, so I can follow changes without losing the account I previously saw.
- As Restaurant staff, I want missing evidence or pending processing to be visible rather than shown as a complete balance, and I want continued access to my history while authorized after deactivation.
- As an MTOGO operator, I want to inspect any Restaurant's statement and trace each amount to its outcome, original date, posting date and preserved terms, so I can reconcile financial results without adding new operational correction actions.

## Agreed decisions

| Question                             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1: Smallest useful journey          | Restaurant staff get a read-only Statements page in the partner app, with month selection, food credits, commission, service fees, adjustments, balance and itemized evidence. Operators view the same information for any Restaurant in Medusa Admin. Exports and manual financial adjustments are deferred.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Q2: Past statements                  | The current Copenhagen calendar month is provisional. It closes automatically at the month boundary and stays fixed. Later adjustments appear in the month they are recorded and link to the original statement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Q3: Recognition month                | Successful delivery establishes the month of food credit and commission. Prepared-food compensation belongs to the month of the qualifying cancellation. Payment alone does not earn commission.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Q4: Commercial terms                 | Preserve the commission terms agreed when successful payment places the Order, together with purchased amounts. Delivery determines the recognition month, not a new commission rate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Q5: Delayed processing               | When a qualifying outcome belongs to an already closed month, record its missing financial entry once as an adjustment in the month recovered. Link the original month and preserve the actual outcome date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Q6: Reduced refund scope             | Preserve #196's reduced scope. Retain original commission amounts and verify the full-reversal calculation with financial tests, without introducing a delivered-Order refund journey. This limits the MVP evidence for #52's full-refund acceptance criterion and must remain explicit in the later specification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Q7: Monthly balance                  | Balance is that month's food credits minus commission minus service fee plus signed adjustments. Do not carry earlier balances into it. Running amounts owed and payment reconciliation are deferred.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Q8: Missing evidence                 | Mark a historical month incomplete when required evidence is missing. Show known entries, withhold affected totals and the final balance, and never infer past active days from current status or report unknown fees as zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Q9: Incomplete closure               | Close a month with missing evidence as incomplete and retain its warning. Reliably established missing amounts appear once as linked adjustments in the current month. Manual historical repair tools are deferred.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Q10: Provisional service fee         | Include only days already active, including today, without forecasting future participation. Show active-day count and calculation. An active Restaurant owes the fee even without Orders; temporary closure remains billable. A fully inactive month with no other activity shows zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Q11: Independent rounding            | Calculate food credit and commission independently from the exact VAT-exclusive purchased food value, then round each final amount under ADR-0006. Monthly totals sum recorded amounts rather than recalculating commission on an aggregate or rounded food credit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Q12: Timeout date                    | The unassigned cancellation deadline establishes the compensation's original month. If recovery processes it after that month closed, actual processing determines the late adjustment's posting month. Preserve both dates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Q13: Bonus handoff                   | Preserve distinct commission, MTOGO-funded compensation and reversal evidence, original outcome dates and posting months. Feature #53 applies its own agreed bonus rules and reads refund obligations. Keep service fees separate; the monthly Restaurant balance is not Bonus pool funding.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Q14: Supported outcomes              | Calculate from explicitly supported outcomes and evidence. Contact attempts or overdue alerts establish neither responsibility nor food-credit entitlement. Consume Operator cancellation evidence when the #49 journey is delivered; do not implement it here. Record the responsibility-table coverage limits.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Q15: Inactive Restaurant access      | Authorized staff retain access to their own Restaurant's statements after deactivation. Revoked staff lose access immediately. Participation and authorization remain separate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Q16: Statement evidence              | Entries show Order reference, outcome, original date, posting date, food credit, commission and explanation. Service fees show active days and calculation; adjustments link to the original month. Provisional/closed status is separate from incomplete or pending-processing warnings. Partner text is Danish and English; Admin is English. Entries are paginated but totals cover the whole month.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Q17: Reliable processing             | Repeated events, concurrency, refund retries and restarts preserve one financial entry per entitlement and one service fee per Restaurant/month. Recover unfinished processing automatically. Known outstanding processing is visible and withholds affected balances. Recovery preserves closed statements and uses late adjustments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Q18: Common policy                   | Use the existing common MVP terms and preserve the version and amounts needed to explain each charge. No terms editor or configurable pricing engine is introduced. Future policy changes must respect Q4.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Q19: Verification and realistic data | Use calculation and HTTP integration tests for precision, month boundaries, missing evidence, ownership, duplicates and recovery. The revised reporting dataset is 100 synthetic Customers and 500 Orders. Any later generator must produce realistic people and coherent Order histories. Measure statement reads under twenty simultaneous journeys against the shared performance baseline. Manually check both partner languages, narrow/wide layouts, keyboard access and long values. Use existing browser verification; add no new Playwright specs for discovery. |

## Accepted examples

| Scenario                                                                                  | Expected behavior                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An Order is paid on 30 November and delivered on 1 December                               | Its delivered food credit and commission belong to December.                                                                                                   |
| A qualifying prepared-food cancellation occurs in December                                | Its compensation belongs to December.                                                                                                                          |
| A later adjustment concerns a closed November statement                                   | November stays fixed. The adjustment appears in the month recorded and links to the original statement.                                                        |
| Restaurant staff inspect a month                                                          | They see only their Restaurant's statement, its totals and evidence. Operators can inspect any Restaurant in Medusa Admin.                                     |
| Commission terms change between successful placement and delivery                         | Use the terms preserved at placement and recognize the earned amount in the delivery month.                                                                    |
| November delivery is financially processed in December after November closed              | Keep November fixed and record one December adjustment with the original outcome date and a link to November.                                                  |
| A month has DKK 600 food credit, DKK 30 commission, DKK 50 service fee and no adjustments | Monthly balance is DKK 520 excluding VAT; it includes no balance from previous months.                                                                         |
| Historical active days cannot be established                                              | Show the known entries and an incomplete state. Service fee and final balance are unavailable, rather than zero.                                               |
| A month closes with missing evidence and a missing amount is later verified               | Preserve the closed incomplete statement and its warning; record the verified missing amount once as a linked current-month adjustment.                        |
| An active Restaurant has no Orders and is temporarily closed                              | Its active days remain billable; the current month shows only days already active, with no forecast of future participation.                                   |
| Purchased food totals DKK 50.31 including VAT                                             | Exact VAT-exclusive food value is DKK 40.248. Food credit rounds to DKK 40.25 and commission to DKK 2.41; do not calculate commission from the rounded credit. |
| An unassigned timeout is due on 30 November but processed on 1 December                   | The compensation refers to November's deadline, with a December adjustment and its actual processing date.                                                     |
| A contact attempt or overdue alert exists without a qualifying terminal outcome           | It does not establish fault or create a financial entitlement by itself.                                                                                       |
| A Restaurant is deactivated but its staff member is still authorized                      | The member can read their Restaurant's statements; revoking authorization removes that access on the next request.                                             |
| A Restaurant is active for fifteen dates of a thirty-day month                            | Service fee is DKK 50 excluding VAT, including dates temporarily closed for new Orders.                                                                        |
| A Restaurant is activated, deactivated and reactivated on one Copenhagen date             | Count that date once. A separate date active at any point counts separately.                                                                                   |
| A Restaurant is active for one day in February 2026, or one day in February 2028          | The independently rounded service fees are DKK 3.57 for a 28-day month and DKK 3.45 for a 29-day month.                                                        |
| An Order's exact food value excluding VAT is DKK 100, 500, 600, 1,000 or 1,100            | Marginal commission is respectively DKK 6, 26, 30, 46 or 49.                                                                                                   |
| An original DKK 30 commission is fully reversed in a financial calculation test           | Reverse DKK 30 using the original amount. This does not claim that a delivered-Order refund journey is available.                                              |

## Source evidence

- The agreed domain model, glossary, case, relevant ADRs and [recipe compatibility review](/MTGO/architecture/restaurant-recipe-review/) were read. The recipe does not establish MTOGO's commercial policy.
- Existing Restaurant participation history records activation and deactivation, separately from opening. Its introduction did not reconstruct earlier participation. Current active status alone cannot establish every historical billable day.
- Existing prepared-food credit records cover MTOGO-funded unassigned-cancellation compensation, with zero commission. They do not record every delivered Restaurant entitlement. Successful handover has a durable outcome and time, but delivered food credit and commission posting still belong to Feature #52.
- Restaurant agreements retain the common terms version, but an Order does not yet snapshot that agreement or commission policy. Purchased prices and quantities belong to native Order lines.
- Refund obligations and attempt history preserve Customer amounts and outcomes. These are distinct from Restaurant credits and commission reversals.
- [Discovery #196](/MTGO/product/operator-refunds/) defers goodwill refunds and corrections. Operator cancellation is agreed for accepted, unfinished Orders; delivered-Order cancellation is refused. In the source reviewed for this round, refund history and retry are delivered, while Operator cancellation remains a separate prerequisite. Feature #52 must distinguish agreed journeys from available implementation.
- The generic Delivery terminal boundary supports a failed outcome, but the reviewed production callers do not establish a Customer-caused failed handover or a responsibility classification. Arrival, contact attempts and attention alone cannot supply that financial evidence.
- Existing staff authorization checks authorized status and own-Restaurant scope independently of participation. Revocation is checked on each request. Statement access follows this established pattern, while its endpoints remain to be implemented.

## MVP coverage and boundaries

The responsibility table in ADR-0006 remains the business rule. This discovery does not introduce new operational failure, goodwill, correction or delivered-Order refund actions. Deliveries and explicit cancellation evidence determine the executable financial journeys; a failure category without responsibility evidence must not be guessed into a credit.

The later specification must distinguish three kinds of acceptance evidence: financial calculation tests, journeys supported by delivered operational Features, and journeys still deferred or dependent on #49. In particular, verifying reversal of an original commission in a financial test does not demonstrate an available delivered-Order refund journey. The same distinction applies to Customer-caused failed-handover credits. These limits must stay visible when assessing Feature #52; discovery does not mark those journeys delivered.

No bank transfers, tax invoicing, payroll, partial refunds, exports, manual financial adjustments, manual historical repair interface, running amount owed, new commercial-policy editor or agent bonus calculation are added here.

## Verification and data handoff

Q19 explicitly replaces the previous 50,000-Customer/200,000-Order reporting verification target with **100 synthetic Customers and 500 Orders**. This smaller exercise dataset does not establish the future capacity described in the assignment. [ADR-0014](/MTGO/architecture/decisions/0014-durable-business-state-and-auditable-corrections/) and [product acceptance](/MTGO/quality/product-acceptance/) record the revised shared target. The twenty-journey concurrency and response-time baselines remain.

If implementation needs a seed script, its Customers and Orders must look realistic: plausible varied names, contact details and Danish delivery addresses; recognizable Restaurant menus, sensible prices, quantities and totals; and coherent placement, acceptance, preparation, delivery, cancellation and refund histories. Use varied dates across statement months and qualifying outcomes to exercise the agreed examples. Boundary and recovery fixtures must remain identifiable, reproducible and consistent with their supporting evidence. Avoid numbered placeholder people and identical artificial purchases. No seed script is implemented during discovery.

The later specification must trace calculation tests, controlled-clock HTTP tests, role checks and recovery evidence to the accepted examples. Financial totals must reconcile on the revised dataset. Measure statement reads against the shared baseline of 95% within two seconds under twenty simultaneous journeys, reporting the workload and timings. Manual UI verification covers Danish and English in the partner app, English in Admin, narrow and wide widths, keyboard access and long values. Existing application verification owns browser automation.

## Shared-understanding handoff

All nineteen decision questions have answers. There are no parked business disagreements. The team explicitly confirmed shared understanding on 24 November 2026; the grill is complete.

The later specification must retain the explicit operational coverage limits, check the installed Medusa APIs for the chosen mapping, and define a reliable financial recognition and close mechanism. Those are implementation obligations, not permission to change the agreed dates, rounding, completeness or access rules. Publishing a specification and creating implementation work items remain separate team invocations. Keep Feature #52 open until its accepted business scope and all required work items are delivered.
