---
title: Product acceptance and FURPS
description: Agreed cross-cutting acceptance rules and exercise validation targets for MTOGO Features.
---

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 business review. These requirements apply to the relevant [Feature stories](/MTGO/product/feature-backlog-proposal/) and their eventual work items. They describe required behavior and evidence; Feature grills choose implementation and test setup. The application has not yet been verified against these targets.

| Area                   | Agreed requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Where it is demonstrated                                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Functionality          | Follow the shared glossary, accepted business rules, role boundaries and responsibility/refund rules.                                                                                                                     | Each Feature's user stories and business-rule acceptance examples, linked to the relevant ADRs.                   |
| Usability              | Support Danish and English, show accurate timeline progress, preserve Customer review when prices/availability change, and distinguish provisional from final financial information.                                      | Menu, checkout, timeline, feedback and financial journeys for the relevant actor.                                 |
| Reliability            | Preserve paid Orders, responsibilities and financial history across restarts; recover expired deadlines; repeated submissions and competing claims preserve the no-duplicate-payment and single-agent guarantees.         | Recovery and concurrent-action examples in ordering, preparation, delivery and operator handling.                 |
| Reliability and access | Enforce ownership, retain reasoned correction history, and keep failed payments/refunds/notifications visible until resolved.                                                                                             | Unauthorized cross-party attempts, corrections and unresolved-failure journeys.                                   |
| Performance            | Twenty simultaneous user journeys; ordinary browsing/status requests within two seconds for 95% of requests; submission within five seconds with an immediately responding mock payment provider.                         | Relevant browsing, status and checkout evidence under the explicitly stated exercise workload.                    |
| Performance and growth | Verify management and financial reporting with 50,000 synthetic Customers and 200,000 historical Orders.                                                                                                                  | Fees/statements, bonus calculations where relevant and management reporting, with stated populations and periods. |
| Supportability         | Every Feature needs user stories refined with an agent before implementation, acceptance examples, linked ADRs and current documentation. Changes to business rules require explicit agreement and documentation updates. | Feature brief, later Feature grill, spec/work-item traceability and delivered evidence.                           |
| Extensibility boundary | Complaint management is outside the current project; future capability must be addable without replacing the agreed core concepts and history.                                                                            | Later Feature design follows the existing module-isolation rules and records meaningful trade-offs.               |

These exercise baselines are testable assumptions to revisit with evidence, rather than production service guarantees. The bonus policy bounds award funding after refunds and MTOGO food losses; actual profitability remains unproven because salary and operating-cost figures are not supplied.

The [Definition of Done](/MTGO/way-of-working/definition-of-done/) and [way of working](/MTGO/way-of-working/how-we-work/) retain their existing review status. This page does not approve unrelated issue #9 policies. Existing deployment, coverage, static-analysis and logging work remains in its original issues; applicable requirements will be traced through Feature parents and their later vertical work items.

See [ADR-0014](/MTGO/architecture/decisions/0014-durable-business-state-and-auditable-corrections/) for the reliability and validation baseline, and the [domain model](/MTGO/product/domain-model/) for business rules.
