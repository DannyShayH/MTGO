Published 16 November 2026. [GitHub release](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/releases/tag/0.1.0).

The first versioned release of MTOGO establishes the foundation for Restaurant discovery, Customer accounts and Restaurant menu management.

## Available features

- Browse Restaurants by postcode or town.
- Register and sign in, verify an email address, and manage Customer names, delivery phone numbers and saved addresses.
- Keep language preferences across visits and devices.
- Preserve separate baskets across Customer session changes.
- Review delivery and contact details before continuing checkout.
- Restaurant staff can sign in to the partner app, open or close their Restaurant, and manage bilingual categories and dishes.
- Manage dish availability, allergens, food labels and photos.
- Operators can manage Restaurant activation and staff access.

## Delivery and quality

- Automated deployment to DigitalOcean staging.
- Automated quality checks and documented SemVer release practices.

## Current scope

MTOGO remains under development. This release establishes the current baseline; it does not represent a complete ordering and delivery service.
