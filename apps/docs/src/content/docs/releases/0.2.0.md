Published 2026-11-17. [GitHub release](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/releases/tag/0.2.0).

Customers now have a personal account with verified email, saved delivery
details and private Order history. This completes Feature #45.

## What Customers can do

- Register and sign in to an isolated Customer account.
- Verify an email address before entering checkout.
- Keep a language preference across visits and devices.
- Keep separate baskets through sign-in and sign-out.
- Edit the Customer name and delivery phone number.
- Manage saved delivery addresses with an optional default.
- Review and keep the checkout destination, contact details and language.
- Recover a forgotten password; old sessions are signed out.
- Change the login email after confirming the password and the new mailbox.
  Login stays usable if the change is interrupted or a notification fails.
- Browse a private, paginated Order history. Other Customers cannot see it, and
  Order transfers are disabled.

## Current scope

Ordering and payment are completed in the next release. MTOGO remains under
development.
