Published 2026-11-20. [GitHub release](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/releases/tag/0.4.0).

Restaurant staff can accept or reject paid Orders and record preparation,
and Customers follow their food's progress. This completes Feature #47.
Delivery agents can also claim and complete deliveries, and the team can
inspect backend requests in Grafana; both are still in progress.

## What Restaurant staff can do

- See paid Orders for their own Restaurant, with the purchased dish details
  in both languages, kept live through connection failures and with an alert
  for each new Order.
- Accept an Order before its five-minute deadline, or reject it. A rejection
  cancels the Order and attempts a full refund to the Customer.
- Record when preparation starts and when the food is ready, without waiting
  for a Delivery agent.

## What Customers can do

- Follow a food-progress timeline: Order placed, Restaurant accepted,
  Preparing, Ready for pickup.
- Cancel their own Order before the Restaurant accepts it.
- An Order the Restaurant does not answer within five minutes is cancelled
  automatically, also after a restart. A refund that has not succeeded stays
  visible as unresolved rather than shown as paid back.

## Deliveries (in progress, Feature #48)

- Operators invite Delivery agents and approve their operating areas, and can
  revoke access without losing a delivery that is already under way.
- Agents see eligible deliveries, claim one or are assigned one, open the
  route in the phone's maps app and see the trip distance.
- Pickup can be confirmed only once the food is ready; handover completes the
  delivery, and agents can view their own completed deliveries.
- Operators are alerted to work unassigned for ten minutes and to stalled
  assigned deliveries. Work unassigned for thirty minutes is cancelled and
  its financial obligations are recovered.

## Delivery and quality

- Backend request monitoring (in progress, #167): a private Prometheus
  exporter and a login-protected Grafana dashboard on stage, with request
  rates, early rejections, the 5xx share, p95 latency and the backend
  process's CPU, memory and event-loop delay, and plain "Unavailable" states
  when there is no evidence.
- Faster local backend checks, branch coverage drops caught before the ready
  flow, and exact Order list counts when unpaid provisional Orders exist.

## Current scope

Payment is a mocked external supplier. Delivery and monitoring are still
being completed, and the live delivery map and SMS follow in later releases.
MTOGO remains under development.
