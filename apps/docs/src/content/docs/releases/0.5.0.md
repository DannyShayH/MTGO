Published 2026-11-23. [GitHub release](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/releases/tag/0.5.0).

Delivery agents can now take a delivery from claim to handover on their
phone, and the Customer sees where the food is on its way. This completes
Feature #48. Restaurant staff can search their Orders and see them by date,
Customers get clearer feedback when they add a dish and at checkout, and the
team's request monitoring warns about unusual traffic.

## What Delivery agents can do

- Record arrival at the Customer's door, and that they tried to reach a
  Customer who does not answer. Ten minutes after arrival without handover,
  an operator is alerted, and the delivery stays the agent's.
- Keep working through a weak connection. The workspace says whether it is
  live, reconnecting or checking every 15 seconds, and catches up when the
  phone is unlocked or back online.
- Share their position after pickup, so the Customer can follow the food.
  The Customer always sees how old the position is, and sharing pauses while
  the phone is locked or another app is open.

## What Restaurant staff can do

- Search Orders by number and filter History by period (today, yesterday,
  the last 7 or 30 days, or chosen dates) and by why the Order was cancelled.
- See the date and time on every History row, and an Order's steps as a
  timeline with the next step, such as Accept or Start preparing, at its end.
- Keep the sound for new Orders switched on. The choice is remembered on the
  device, and after a reload the first tap on the page lets it play.

## What Customers can do

- Add a dish with one smooth confirmation: the button becomes a quantity
  control once, and the basket count changes at the tap.
- Go through checkout without getting stuck: it always opens the next
  unfinished step, shows the delivery address on the delivery step, and the
  pay button shows the total and stays clear while the payment goes through.
- See what is left to fill in on their profile, with a link to each part.

## Delivery and quality

- Each pickup ships from its own Restaurant's stock location.
- Operators can see who triggered each refund attempt (part of Feature #49).
- Request monitoring (#167, in progress): streams and interrupted requests no
  longer count as slow API calls, low-traffic panels say so per viewer, and
  high-traffic warnings have tested limits.
- Flow metrics keep a Feature's timing when work removed from it never
  started.

## Current scope

Payment is a mocked external supplier. Delivered Orders still stay under
"In progress" for the Restaurant until #204 moves them to History. Operator
exception handling (#49) and the Customer's live timeline (#50) follow in
later releases. MTOGO remains under development.
