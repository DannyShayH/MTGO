Published 2026-11-26. [GitHub release](https://github.com/DannyShayH/MTGO/releases/tag/0.6.0).

Customers can now follow their Order live on a map and rate it once it is
delivered, and operators can resolve problems and refund Orders in full. This
completes Features #49, #50 and #51. Restaurant staff see their Orders move to
History when the food is delivered, and their first monthly statements show
food credits, commission and the service fee.

## What Customers can do

- Follow an Order from the confirmation page or from Order history, in the
  language the Order was placed in: preparation, assignment, pickup, handover,
  and cancellation or failed delivery when that happens.
- See the Delivery agent's position on a map after pickup, with how old the
  position is and how accurate it was. A stale position says so.
- Get updates as they happen. The page reconnects by itself after a lost
  connection or a locked phone, and checks every ten seconds when live
  updates are not available.
- See whether a refund is still on its way, separately from the delivery.
- Rate the food, the overall experience and the Delivery agent from 1 to 5,
  once, within seven days of delivery. The tracking page invites them while
  Feedback is open.
- Open the Restaurant menu in under a second.

## What Restaurant staff can do

- See a delivered Order move from In progress to History.
- See the financial terms an Order was bought on, kept as they were even when
  the menu changes later.
- Read this month's statement: food credits and commission for delivered
  Orders, the service fee for each active day, prepared-food compensation for
  cancelled Orders, and the monthly balance. Closed months stay fixed, and a
  late change shows as an adjustment in the current month with both dates.
- Receive the staff invitation by email, not only as a copied link.

## What Delivery agents can do

- Report a problem with the current delivery, with a category and an
  optional note. An operator sees it at once, and the delivery stays theirs.

## What operators can do

- Cancel an accepted Order with a full refund and a required reason. If the
  kitchen had started, the Restaurant is compensated for the prepared food.
- See failed and pending refunds with every attempt, and retry a failed one
  safely.
- Compare Restaurant and Delivery agent ratings for the last 30 days, the
  previous month or all time in Medusa Admin.

## Delivery and quality

- Feedback and Restaurant statements are released with feature flags
  (ADR-0022) and switched on on stage.
- Request monitoring (#167, in progress): every team member has a Grafana
  Viewer account on stage. The dashboards show host memory and disk, keep
  their history through releases and rollbacks, and are easier to read at a
  glance. Application logs can be searched across all three apps by request
  ID, and logging covers more than partner sign-in.
- A demo seed creates 500 rated Orders for Feedback.
- The docs site deploy retries when GitHub is slow to hand over the build.

## Current scope

Payment is a mocked external supplier. Restaurant statements (#52) are in
progress: inspecting statements in Medusa Admin and recovering after
interrupted processing follow. Delivery agent bonuses (#53) are built behind
a flag that is off on stage. Accepting the monitoring against the
non-functional requirements (#184) and alert emails (#248) complete #167
later. MTOGO remains under development.
