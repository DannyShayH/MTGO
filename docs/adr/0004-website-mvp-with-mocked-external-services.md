# A website MVP with mocked payment and SMS services

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. The MVP covers website journeys for Customers, Restaurant staff, Delivery agents and MTOGO operators in Danish and English, using mocked external payment and SMS services that demonstrate both successful and failed outcomes. Native apps, telephone ordering and scheduled orders are outside the MVP so the team can deliver a coherent ordering-to-feedback journey before adding channels; complaint management remains outside the project as required by the case.

The customer website includes an Order timeline carrying the same business progress information as mocked SMS. Agent release and reassignment, both before and after pickup, are outside the MVP by team choice. Dish options, meal customization, promotions and tips are also outside the MVP. This decision establishes the product boundary and does not prescribe feature implementation; the evolving MoSCoW Feature backlog is agreed in the product documentation, with optional opening schedules and arrival estimates as Could have candidates.

Extended on 12 November 2026 in the Feature #45 grill (discovery #69): verification, password-reset and login-email-change messages use a private test mailbox for the demonstrable MVP, deferring real outbound email. Exercise the actual links, expiry, resending and delivery failures through captured messages; recovery links must not be publicly exposed and email verification must not be bypassed. This keeps the complete account journey demonstrable within the exercise's mocked-service boundary.

## Discovery #152 revision: browser updates and delivery location

On 18 November 2026 the team replaced the mocked-SMS scope with browser SSE in Feature #50 and added actual phone location sharing from pickup until delivery completion. Feature #48 captures agent location and delivery actions; #50 provides the ordering Customer with a live timeline and delivery map, including the location last-updated time. Location permission is required. Pickup and handover remain explicit agent confirmations, never inferred from coordinates. This supersedes the SMS requirement above; mocked payment remains in scope. The website MVP and separate partner app remain. The team accepted foreground browser tracking: location may pause while the phone is locked or another app is open, with latest-location age shown to the Customer. Denied permission or unavailable location does not prevent delivery completion. Retain only the latest active-delivery coordinate and remove it when the delivery ends; preserve business milestones.
