# Separate role access and Restaurant participation from opening

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. Customers may browse freely but must sign in before submitting an Order, and access only their own Orders. MTOGO operators create Restaurants and Delivery agent access; Restaurant staff operate only their own Restaurant, while agents see available work in their operating area and their assigned deliveries. Customer contact details become visible to an agent only after assignment, limiting exposure to people responsible for the delivery.

A Restaurant needs explicit activation by MTOGO, a pickup address, contact details, authorized staff, agreed commercial terms, a published menu and a defined delivery area. Participation is active or inactive, separately from open or closed availability. New Orders require an active, open Restaurant serving the destination and offering the selected items; the MVP uses manual opening decisions and postal-code delivery areas. Closing or deactivating a Restaurant does not abandon existing Orders, and revoking access preserves historical Orders and responsibilities. Automatic opening schedules are outside the initial manual-opening journey.

Operators manage Restaurant staff access, agent access, operating areas, activation/deactivation and commercial terms. Restaurant staff manage their own menus, opening availability, acceptance and preparation, and may see only their Restaurant's commercial statements. Agents claim work and report progress only for assigned deliveries; they may see their own completed deliveries, points and provisional/final bonus amounts, clearly distinguished. Operators handle refunds and reasoned corrections but cannot impersonate a Customer's Feedback. Neither Restaurant staff nor agents can see another Restaurant's or agent's financial details.
