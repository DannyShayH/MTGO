# Place paid Orders before acceptance and find agents alongside preparation

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. The Customer is charged at submission, and successful payment immediately creates an Order awaiting Restaurant acceptance, independently of agent assignment. Restaurant rejection or failure to accept within five minutes cancels the Order and requires a full refund; the Order and payment history remain, and a failed refund stays visibly unresolved until successful. Uncertain payment results remain pending while checked, and retrying must not create another purchase or charge.

After acceptance, food preparation and delivery assignment proceed independently. Agents claim available deliveries in their operating area, with at most one active delivery each; competing claims produce one responsible agent, and MTOGO operators can intervene in initial assignment and exception handling. Pickup requires both ready food and an assigned agent. This deliberately changes the recipe's sequence, which waits for an agent claim before Order creation and preparation, so kitchen progress and the existence of a paid Order do not depend on a claim. ADR-0011 records unclaimed work, cancellation and handover failures. Agent release and reassignment are outside the MVP, including after pickup, by explicit team choice.
