# Bound bonuses by commission and distinguish feedback subjects

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. The monthly Delivery agent bonus pool is capped at 20% of positive commission remaining after refunds, reversals, MTOGO-funded food compensation and carried losses, excluding service fees. A zero or negative available balance produces no pool, and unused budget stays with MTOGO. This tightens the original commission-only cap after agreeing that MTOGO bears prepared-food losses. DKK 1,000 commission minus DKK 300 compensation allows at most DKK 140 in the pool. The cap bounds bonus expense but does not prove overall profitability because salaries and other operating costs are unspecified.

Only the ordering Customer may submit voluntary Feedback after successful delivery, with three separate integer ratings from 1 to 5 for food, overall experience and the individual Delivery agent. There is one submission per delivered Order, within seven days of delivery, and it cannot be edited in the MVP. Missing feedback is unrated, never zero. Only the agent rating may affect an individual's rating-based bonus; food ratings must not reduce that agent's reward, because responsibility for the food and its delivery differ.

## Pool allocation

Base points for each successfully delivered Order equal its value excluding VAT. Multiply by 1.25 if pickup occurs in Copenhagen early hours (06:00 to before 09:00) or late hours (21:00 to before 06:00 the next day), and by 1.10 for an agent rating of 4 or 5. Other agent ratings and missing Feedback retain the baseline. A DKK 200 Order picked up at 22:00 with a five-star agent rating earns 275 points; monetary awards share the available pool proportionally to each agent's points.

Finalize the previous month's pool on the 8th so the seven-day Feedback window can finish. Recognize approved refund obligations even while the provider refund remains unresolved. Fully refunded Orders before finalization earn no points. Later refunds reduce future available funding and negative funding balances carry forward; previously finalized awards are not deducted from employees' wages. Round monetary awards down to increments of DKK 0.01 and retain the rounding remainder. Agents can distinguish their provisional points and amounts from finalized awards and can see only their own delivery and bonus information.
