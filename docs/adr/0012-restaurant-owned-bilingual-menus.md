# Restaurants publish their own bilingual menus

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. Restaurant staff publish their own menu without MTOGO approving individual dishes, preserving the case's menu-ownership boundary. Category and dish names require Danish and English; optional descriptions require both languages when supplied. Restaurants provide allergen information and missing information is visibly unknown. Prices are positive DKK amounts including VAT and delivery. Saving valid changes makes them visible immediately; sold-out dishes remain visible but cannot be ordered.

Prices and availability are checked again before payment. A change requires the Customer to review the updated basket, never silent substitution or an increased charge. Removing menu content preserves historical Orders. Dish options, meal customization, promotions and tips are outside the MVP. ADR-0006 records the exercise's 25% menu-VAT assumption and commission rounding.

Refined on 13 November 2026 in Feature #44 discovery #102: each Restaurant owns flat categories, each dish belongs to exactly one category, and staff explicitly control sold-out status without stock quantities or daily resets. Refuse removal of an active Restaurant's last published dish; staff may mark it sold out, but an operator must deactivate the Restaurant before its last published dish can be removed. This preserves the published-menu activation boundary without automatically changing participation or opening status. Active closed menus remain readable; inactive menus are hidden from public browsing. A successful valid save publishes on the next menu fetch, with stale edits refused rather than silently overwriting newer changes. The menu workflow page records the detailed food declarations, secure optional photos and acceptance examples.

Final discovery confirmation adds a shared allergen catalogue seeded into the database during normal initial setup, with stable unique EU Annex II numbers 1–14 and Danish/English names and descriptions. Keep the catalogue fixed for the MVP, without catalogue-management screens; future reviewed changes preserve existing dish references. Halal, chili and garlic are separate fixed food labels, without EU allergen numbers. Numbered selection and trusted SVG symbols take UX inspiration from the team's earlier project, while implementation follows Medusa, the MTOGO design brief and the required `emil-design-eng` and `frontend-design` skills.
