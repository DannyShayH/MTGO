# Restaurants publish their own bilingual menus

Agreed on 11 November 2026 in the issue #10 domain review. Restaurant staff publish their own menu without MTOGO approving individual dishes, preserving the case's menu-ownership boundary. Category and dish names require Danish and English; optional descriptions require both languages when supplied. Restaurants provide allergen information and missing information is visibly unknown. Prices are positive DKK amounts including VAT and delivery. Saving valid changes makes them visible immediately; sold-out dishes remain visible but cannot be ordered.

Prices and availability are checked again before payment. A change requires the Customer to review the updated basket, never silent substitution or an increased charge. Removing menu content preserves historical Orders. Dish options, meal customization, promotions and tips are outside the MVP. ADR-0006 records the exercise's 25% menu-VAT assumption and commission rounding.
