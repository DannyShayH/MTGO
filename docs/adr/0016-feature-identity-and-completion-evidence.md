# Recognize Features explicitly and require completion evidence

Agreed during discovery #57 for Feature #42. A Feature uses the mandatory title prefix `Feature: `, independently of whether it has children; native direct sub-issue relationships identify its work items, while other structural parents remain excluded from work-item metrics without becoming Features. This extends ADR-0007 using the existing backlog convention rather than adding classification labels or a separate project field; the prefix is therefore part of the reporting contract.

Every direct child, including discovery, is required unless the team explicitly removes it from scope and records why, and work items do not have further children. Publish completed Feature lead time only when the parent is closed as completed and every required work item is delivered; the measured finish remains the latest required work-item delivery, even if acceptance is recorded later. A premature parent completion or a missing Start date on a started or delivered child is a visible data problem, not permission to report a guessed or partial duration.

Scope removal normally keeps the child linked, records the reason on the parent and closes abandoned work as not planned; already delivered work retains its delivery history. Preserve the original Feature start through removal or movement, and make unavailable historical evidence visible instead of silently shortening the duration. A recognized Feature remains a Feature if its title loses the prefix, with a naming data problem rather than a change in counting.

Reopened required work invalidates completed Feature lead time until delivery and renewed acceptance, using the original start and eventual latest required finish. A wholly withdrawn Feature closes as not planned and has no completed Feature lead time, while its delivered children remain delivered work items. Progress is the count of delivered required work items out of required work items, including discovery, rather than a claim about the percentage of business capability implemented.

Reconstruct Feature identity and membership from GitHub's retained events and preserved Start dates, including former children and archived Feature evidence, rather than introducing board snapshots or automatic repository commits. This preserves ADR-0002; missing evidence makes the affected timing unavailable instead of resetting it. A child's actual Start date contributes even when it predates attachment or creation of the Feature, and fetching a child for Feature evidence does not change work-item board-membership rules.
